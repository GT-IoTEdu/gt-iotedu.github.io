About the Project

The exponential growth of IoT devices in institutions has brought new challenges in management, security, and usability. Our solution aims to democratize access to IoT technology in a secure and efficient way.

Current Challenges

Configuration Complexity

Bureaucratic and technical processes that hinder the adoption of IoT devices

Inadequate Security

Lack of standardized protocols for IoT devices on institutional networks

Dependence on IT

Users constantly rely on the technical staff for basic configurations

Our Solution

An intuitive platform that lets users register and manage their IoT devices on their own, with built-in security.

IoTEdu at WRNP and SBRC 2026

We took the project to Praia do Forte (Bahia, Brazil), represented by Joner De Mello Assolin, Diego Kreutz, and Rodrigo Miani. We gave four presentations: two at WRNP 2026 (booth and main stage) and two at the SBRC 2026 Tools Session (Salão de Ferramentas). The highlights were IoT-ID (deterministic device fingerprinting) and IoT-Zoo (a testbed with more than 40 devices for reproducible, large-scale experiments). We came back with valuable feedback and new collaborations, some of them beyond Brazil!

Project Results

Explore the products and tools developed throughout the RNP Working Group.

Testbed

IoT-Zoo

A container-based framework for simulating heterogeneous IoT devices. It automates the deployment of multi-domain scenarios and traffic capture (PCAP) to support research, teaching, and solution validation.

IoT-Zoo Repository
Testbed

AttackZoo

A reproducible environment that integrates a containerized repository of attacks mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. It enables the traceable generation of network traffic datasets for cybersecurity research, teaching, and outreach.

AttackZoo Repository
Proof of Concept

Proof of Concept I

The first stage of the IoT device registration and validation flow: it identifies the device, records metadata, and paves the way for network provisioning and the enforcement of basic security controls.

See how it evolved into IoTEdu Core
Proof of Concept

Proof of Concept II

An evolution of the flow with built-in security: it connects security sensors to the environment and allows firewall policies to be triggered from collected evidence, while maintaining governance and traceability of actions.

See how it evolved into IoTEdu Core
Tool

IoT-ID Fingerprinting

A deterministic IoT device identification tool. It uses traffic analysis to produce stable, explainable identities without relying on probabilistic models or AI training.

IoT-ID Repository
Platform

IoTEdu Core (MVP)

The core that integrates registration with institutional security. It centralizes the collection of signals and alerts from security sensors and, based on them, enables responses via the firewall.

IoTEdu Core Repository

Public Datasets

GT-IoTEdu Attack Dataset

A labeled traffic dataset with 42 attack scenarios in a controlled environment, covering reconnaissance, network exploitation, web attacks, brute force, exfiltration, denial of service, and IoT. Generated by AttackZoo with MITRE ATT&CK mapping.

GT-IoTEdu IoT-Zoo Dataset

A packet-level benign traffic dataset captured on the IoT-Zoo testbed, with 43 heterogeneous device profiles across the Urban Observatory, Industrial, e-Health, and Smart Farming domains. It results from the synchronized merging of Scapy and Tshark captures.
