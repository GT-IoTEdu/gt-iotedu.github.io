1 Who we are

IoTEdu is a service for the management and security of Internet of Things devices on academic networks, developed within GT-IoTEdu, a Working Group supported by the Rede Nacional de Ensino e Pesquisa (RNP, Brazil's National Research and Education Network).

This Privacy Policy applies to the reference instance of the service, available at app-unipampa.iotedu.org, operated by Fundação Universidade Federal do Pampa (UNIPAMPA), a federal public-law foundation registered with the CNPJ/MF (Brazil's National Registry of Legal Entities) under No. 09.341.233/0001-22.

This document has been prepared in accordance with Lei nº 13.709/2018 (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, or LGPD), with Lei nº 12.965/2014 (Marco Civil da Internet) and with the rules for service providers set forth in the Policy of the Comunidade Acadêmica Federada (CAFE.N.001).

IoTEdu is software installed on the infrastructure of each adopting institution. When another institution begins operating its own instance, that institution becomes the controller of the data processed there and is responsible for its own privacy policy. This policy applies exclusively to the instance identified above.

3 Definitions

For the purposes of this policy, the following definitions apply.

Personal data Information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as a name, an institutional email address, or an account identifier.
Processing Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, use, access, storage, transmission, modification, and erasure.
Data subject, user, you The natural person to whom the processed personal data relate. For the purposes of this policy, any person who accesses the service or who is listed as the person responsible for a registered device.
Controller The natural or legal person responsible for decisions regarding the processing of personal data.
Data Protection Officer (Encarregado) The person appointed by the controller to act as a communication channel between the controller, data subjects, and the ANPD.
ANPD Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (Brazil's National Data Protection Authority), responsible for safeguarding, implementing, and enforcing compliance with the LGPD.
CAFe Comunidade Acadêmica Federada (CAFe Federation), RNP's identity management service, which brings together teaching, research, and innovation institutions in Brazil.
Identity provider (IdP) The institution responsible for maintaining the user's credentials and for authenticating the user. In practice, the user's home institution.
Service provider (SP) A component that, based on assertions from an identity provider, controls access to a protected service. IoTEdu acts as a service provider in CAFe.
Device Internet of Things equipment registered on the platform, such as a sensor, camera, controller, or laboratory equipment.
Device owner The user who registers the device and to whom it is linked in the institution's inventory.
Unit A campus, department, laboratory, or other institutional subdivision with which devices and administrators are associated.

4 The IoTEdu service

IoTEdu allows members of the academic community to register, on a self-service basis, the Internet of Things devices they use on the institution's network, and allows the information technology team to maintain an inventory of this equipment, identify the person responsible for each device, monitor its network behavior, and respond to security events.

Authentication is performed using the user's institutional identity through CAFe. The service does not maintain its own passwords for federated users and does not perform authentication: authentication is performed by the user's home institution.

The platform is installed and runs on the institution's infrastructure, on a local server, and monitors a network segment dedicated to Internet of Things devices.

5 Personal data processing agents

The user's home institution, in its capacity as identity provider, maintains the user's credentials and registration data and decides which attributes it releases to the service. With respect to such data, it acts as controller.

UNIPAMPA, in its capacity as operator of the IoTEdu reference instance, makes decisions regarding the processing of data within the service. With respect to such data, it acts as controller.

There are no third-party processors. The service does not use a cloud provider, does not engage external processing services, and does not transfer personal data outside the institution's infrastructure.

7 Legal bases for processing

ProcessingLegal basis
User identification, assignment of access profiles, device inventory, and network security monitoring Execution of public policies by the public administration (LGPD, art. 7º, III), since the management and protection of the institutional network are activities inherent to a federal university
Retention of application access logs Compliance with a legal obligation (LGPD, art. 7º, II), pursuant to art. 15 of the Marco Civil da Internet

Processing is limited to the minimum necessary for these purposes. The data are not used for commercial purposes, advertising, profiling of personal behavior, or evaluation of academic or professional performance.

Indicators produced for GT reports and for scientific publications are aggregated and anonymous, in the form of counts, averages, and response times. No data that identify individual persons or devices are published.

8 Sharing of personal data

The service does not sell, assign, or share personal data with third parties, companies, or other institutions.

Data may be shared when the service receives a request from a competent authority, to comply with a legal or regulatory requirement, and when a security incident must be reported to the CAFe Federation Operator, in which case sharing is limited to what is necessary to handle the incident.

Internally, access is segmented by profile: end users access only their own devices; unit administrators access the devices and events of their unit; the institutional superuser accesses the institution as a whole.

9 Automated decisions

The platform may automatically block a device's access to the network when the detection sensors identify a high-severity event. The decision is executed by the system without prior human intervention, and the device owner is notified. Lower-severity events do not trigger automatic blocking and are forwarded for triage by the unit administrator.

Pursuant to art. 20 of the LGPD, the data subject may request a review of this decision. The request may be submitted to the unit administrator through the platform itself or through the channels indicated in section 2.

10 Storage and retention periods

Data are stored on a local server, on the infrastructure of the institution operating the instance, within Brazilian territory. There is no storage in third-party clouds and no international data transfer.

DataRetention period
User account and attributes received from CAFe For as long as the account exists
Device registration and link to the device owner For as long as the device remains registered
Security events, incidents, and blocking records 12 months, after which they are anonymized or erased
Application access logs 6 months, in accordance with art. 15 of the Marco Civil da Internet

Upon termination of the user's relationship with the institution, or upon a request for deletion, the personal data associated with the account are erased, except for records whose retention is required by law or necessary for the regular exercise of rights, which may be kept for the corresponding period.

11 Security measures

The following technical and organizational measures are adopted in the service:

  • communication exclusively over HTTPS, with a valid certificate;
  • federated authentication, with no storage of user passwords;
  • dedicated network segmentation for monitored devices;
  • profile-based access control, with separation between end user, unit administrator, and institutional superuser;
  • audit logging of administrative actions;
  • infrastructure maintained within the institution itself, subject to its security policies and network controls.

The reference instance operates in the context of a research pilot. This does not reduce the care taken with the data, but it is relevant information for assessing its use. No system can completely eliminate the possibility of a security breach. If you believe that your data are not being processed properly, or that they are exposed to unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or alteration, please contact us through the channels in section 2.

12 User rights

Data subjects are guaranteed the rights provided for in the LGPD. Requests to exercise these rights will only be fulfilled when submitted by the data subject or by a legally authorized representative.

Confirmation and access You may request confirmation of the existence of processing and access to your personal data processed by the service.
Rectification You may request the rectification of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data.
Anonymization, blocking, or erasure You may request the anonymization, blocking, or erasure of data that are excessive, unnecessary, or processed in breach of the LGPD.
Information on sharing You may request a list of the entities with which the service has shared your personal data.
Review of automated decisions You may request a review of the automatic device blocking described in section 9.
Objection You may object to processing carried out in breach of the LGPD.

Some of these rights may be exercised directly on the platform: your account data and your devices are visible on your dashboard, and you may correct the information about a device or remove it from your registration.

Where it is not possible or appropriate to take the requested measures immediately, a response will be sent stating the corresponding factual or legal reasons, as provided for in art. 18, § 4º, of the LGPD.

13 Revision history

This Privacy Policy may be edited, modified, or updated at any time in order to keep it current and consistent with applicable regulations and with privacy and data protection practices.

Changes may occur when there are changes to the data processed, the purposes, or the operations performed, as well as when required by the ANPD or another competent body. Changes affecting the processing of data received from the federation will also be communicated to CAFe.
